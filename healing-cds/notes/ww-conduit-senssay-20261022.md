## Night two, 23 October: what moved

Four keys since the audit: Ruby Life Pools +16 late on the 22nd, then Altar of Fangs +16, Voidscar Arena +16 and Murder Row +16 on the 23rd. Same ruler, plus a +17 reference pulled for each of those dungeons (Boombox, Yoyway, Quxi, Fixus).

- **Fists of Fury: fixed.** Channels run to the last hit 86%, 78% and 90% of the time in the three keys of the 23rd (night one 48 to 55%, references 81 to 97%). Cut by your own press 10, 13 and 7 times (night one 39 to 53), and the cutters are now Karma, Touch of Death, WDP and Conduit rather than Spinning Crane Kick and Tiger Palm at the tail. Damage lost to cut channels fell from about 3% to 0.5 to 1.1%, and the four-piece now averages 5.7 to 5.8 stacks (was 5.3).
- **Four-piece consumer: much better, not done.** Spinning Crane Kick ate it at 3+ targets 81%, 88% and 67% (night one 54 to 68%, references 83 to 100%). RSK or RWK took it 15, 7 and 20 times (night one 26 to 38). Murder Row is the weak one: Rushing Wind Kick right after Fists six times (0:51.0, 3:07.0, 11:32.2, 11:55.6, 13:02.7, 14:05.1) and RSK fourteen times. Rule unchanged: at 2+ targets SCK is the first spender after Fists, RWK and RSK go before the Fists or after that SCK.
- **Potion stat: fixed.** Mastery 1146, haste 941, crit 797, so all 14 potions of the 23rd boosted mastery. Food in Altar and Murder Row, none in Voidscar. Chest enchant is missing on the new chest piece.
- **Zenith Stomps: half fixed, and it created a new leak.** 1.0, 0.84 and 0.95 manual stomps per Zenith (night one 0.2 to 0.95, Ruby 1.47, references 1.3 to 1.9), 20 to 22 charges still expiring per key. The stomps you do press come back to back: Murder Row had 18 mastery breaks, 8 of them Stomp into Stomp or Stomp as the first press after Zenith (8:35.7, 8:36.7, 12:04.9, 13:18.9, 16:44.2, 17:54.3, 20:45.3), Voidscar 14 breaks. One spender between the two stomps, every time.
- **Conduit placement: right spot, wrong length.** Inside the Xuen window 100%, 100%, 91% (good), but the channel now averages 2.1s, 1.6s and 1.9s, and five Conduits ran the full channel (Altar 14:34.5, 19:37.5, 22:43.2; Murder Row 8:32.9, 20:43.4). The guide: always cancel on the next GCD; the references average 1.0 to 1.6s. And the boss-opener habit is still there: Conduit at +0.8s before any WDP at Altar 14:34.5, Voidscar 16:52.5 and 21:44.2, Murder Row 5:03.7 and 10:05.9.
- **Deaths: not better.** Ruby 3, Altar 1, Voidscar 4 (Whirling Gust 9:14, Poison Splash on Atroxus 12:16, Condensed Mass 21:21 and Cosmic Crash 23:04 on Charonus), Murder Row 0. References 0 to 2 per key. In all four Voidscar deaths both defensives had been spent 25 to 88s earlier.
- **Tiger Palm at full energy: unchanged** at 48 to 53% (references 26 to 41%). Press it before the free WDP, Dance SCK, Blackout Kick, RWK chain.
- **Talents:** you went back to the 150-energy build (Inner Peace, no Drinking Horn Cover) for the 23rd. Xuen count is fine on it (11 to 12 per key, references 12 to 16), so either build works; on this one the rule from fix 6 does not bind.
- **The gap to the references** is still wide: 241K to 282K against 412K to 490K at +17 with 329 to 334 item level against your 323. Part of that is gear (ilvl, the two-hander, the set bonus trade), part is deaths and pull size, and the rest is the list above: the remaining four-piece misses, stomps, full Conduit channels and energy capping.

## What to fix, in order

Five keys on 2026-10-22: Voidscar Arena +6, Den of Nalorakk +16, Temple of Sethraliss +16, Kings' Rest +16, The Blinding Vale +16. The two fair references are the top Windwalkers in the same dungeons one key level up, Chumafat (+17 Den, 392K) and Mnkn (+17 Temple, 424K), with Freevalon (+23 Den) and Bisset (+22 Temple) as the ceiling. Every number below was computed from the raw combat events twice, by the audit script and again by an independent recount, and checked against the guide's wording.

1. **After Fists of Fury you press Rising Sun Kick, then Spinning Crane Kick. Reverse it.** Each Fists tick stacks Unbroken Rhythm to 6. The next Rising Sun Kick, Rushing Wind Kick or Spinning Crane Kick eats the stacks: SCK gets +120% on every target, RSK gets +60% on one. At two or more targets you fed the stacks to RSK or RWK on 126 of 330 Fists across the four +16s (38%). Chumafat 5 of 74, Mnkn 1 of 105, Bisset 0 of 134. Den 3:48.6 is the whole habit in four presses: Zenith, Fists with 6 stacks on 5 targets, RSK at 3:50.0 ate it for 216K, SCK at 3:52.0 went out with nothing. On the same pull a 6-stack SCK did 2.69M. The opener is the worst spot: of the Fists cast inside the Zenith window at two or more targets, 18 of 25 went to RSK or RWK, because you run the single-target line (Fists, RSK, WDP) on packs. The pack line is Fists, SCK, RSK, WDP, and the WDP lands one GCD later with nothing lost. Shape to drill: RSK or RWK before Fists, SCK right after it. Single target: RWK if lit, otherwise a Dance of Chi-Ji SCK, RSK only when it unlocks WDP that instant (43 of your 69 single-target RSK consumers did neither). About 4 to 5% of your damage per key.

2. **You cut Fists of Fury 0.4s before its last hit.** Outside Heart of the Jade Serpent a Fists is five tick clusters, the last one due a median 2.88s after the press at your haste (range 2.1 to 3.1s). Your next press lands at a median 2.35s: 126 of 202 slow Fists were cut by your own button (references 2 to 14%). Inside HotJS you wait fine (9 of 179 cut). The last tick is 22% of the Fists because Momentum Boost makes each tick hit harder than the one before, and a cut Fists also leaves 4 or 5 stacks instead of 6 (your average 5.3, the references 5.7 to 5.9). Kings' Rest 7:53.5: hits at 0, 0.72, 1.46, 2.19, Tiger Palm at 2.26 with the fifth hit due at 2.46. The cutting buttons are the rotation itself: SCK, Tiger Palm, RSK, RWK, WDP. Fix: do not use a stopwatch, wait for the channel bar to empty or the sixth hit to land, then press. Tiger Palm goes before Fists, never on its tail. If you mash, add [nochanneling:Fists of Fury] to the filler buttons. Since Kings' Rest the same press has started reaching the fast HotJS channels too (5 of 65 cut there, 12 of 62 in Blinding Vale, the press landing 0.2s after the fourth hit), so the rule holds inside bursts as well. About 3% of your damage per key, plus the lost stacks.

3. **Zenith Stomp charges die unused.** Each Zenith press gives three stomps and spends one; the two manual ones were left on the bar 74 times across Den, Temple and Kings' Rest, 27 times in Voidscar and 19 in Blinding Vale. 37 of your 69 full Zenith windows had zero manual stomps, while Tiger Palm went out at full energy with both charges up (Den 1:10.2 and 1:13.7). The references lose 1 to 5 charges per key and stomp twice in nearly every Zenith. Where they put them: one stomp inside the Celestial Conduit channel (Conduit, Stomp, then Blackout Kick or SCK ends the channel) or as the first filler after WDP, the other where Tiger Palm would go. Their two stomps sit 3 to 6s apart; your 11 double stomps were 1.07s apart, which is Stomp into Stomp and breaks mastery (five breaks in Temple). Never stomp as the first press after Zenith either, the free stomp counts. Five Xuen windows had no Zenith at all because the charge went out 3 to 10s before Xuen was ready (Den 8:38 and 22:29, Temple 2:00, 5:03 and 20:38). About 2 to 4% of your damage per key, plus 2 chi per stomp.

4. **Conduit goes out before the window's first Whirling Dragon Punch.** The Unity Within window from the Conduit cancel and the WDP window do not stack. Blinding Vale 9:38.4: Xuen, Zenith, Conduit at +0.7, RSK, WDP at +2.5 with 5s of Unity still running, so one of the two windows was thrown away. Same at Blinding Vale 4:59.1, Kings' Rest 12:10.2, Temple 14:55.9 and Den 8:48.7. The order is Zenith, Fists, SCK, RSK, WDP, then Conduit as the WDP window ends (about +11 to +13), cancelled on the next GCD. Zenith and WDP also went out 3 to 10s before Xuen was ready in 10 of 33 bursts, so those bursts opened without one or both HotJS windows; Temple 5:03.6 did 10.45M, the next Xuen on the same pull did 19.77M. Xuen is the clock: from 20s before it is ready, Zenith and WDP are locked.

5. **Eight deaths in five keys. The four references had one between them.** Merektha 9:53.5: Burrow hit for 918K, then 2.3s of nothing with Touch of Karma, Fortifying Brew and Spear Hand Strike all ready, then an interruptible Poison Spit killed you. Dead 151.5s of a 235s fight, 11% of that key's damage. Orb Watcher 17:32 and 17:57: two Loose Sparks one-shots (1.14M unmitigated against 801K HP) with Fortifying Brew ready both times; with Fort up each hit was survivable. Karma and Fort were pressed within 3s of each other five times (references 0 of 42 Karmas), then the repeat 22 to 35s later killed you with both down (Kings' Rest Bladestorm 7:39, Blinding Vale Light Bolt 1:11). Loa Speaker Nanea: four pulses of Volatile Flames, a Fists channelled inside them, Earthquake taken at 62%. Rule: one defensive the moment a hit drops you under about 60%, hold the other for the repeat; kick Poison Spit; Fort before crossing Sparks; Vivify under 35% when both are down.

6. **On the new build, Zenith outside Xuen costs you Xuens.** Since Kings' Rest you run Drinking Horn Cover, and on that build both Zenith charges recharge on a flat 90s, the same clock as Xuen (all 17 Kings' Rest Zenith presses fit that replay). Every one of your 9 Kings' Rest Xuens went out within 0.3s of a Zenith, so a Zenith spent outside Xuen delays the next Xuen until a charge is back. Six of your eight off-Xuen Zeniths in Kings' Rest went out 0.9 to 29.6s before Xuen was ready (4:00.4, 7:01.1, 15:01.3, 18:34.7, 20:59.9, 24:01.9), each left zero charges, and Xuen then waited 38 to 179s. That is 208 damage presses with Xuen ready and no Zenith, against 3 for Chumafat and 28 for Mnkn; Kings' Rest got 9 Xuens where the +17s got 13 and 14 in keys of the same length. The burst macro makes it worse: it is keyed on Zenith, so at 18:34.7 and 24:01.9 the potion and Zenith fired a second or three before Xuen and the trinket were ready, and 5 of your 21 potions went out with no Xuen at all. Rule: after the pull double, Zenith only inside a Xuen window; never press the macro before Xuen is off cooldown; press Xuen the moment it is up, not when the next Zenith charge is. This one comes from the completeness pass, its numbers are that agent's own recount, and it is worth about 5% of the Kings' Rest damage by its estimate.
