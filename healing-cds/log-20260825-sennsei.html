Priority Fixes (ranked by impact)

1 — CriticalAnchor Divine Hymn to boss timers, not to how scary the frames look
The single most expensive habit of the night. Twin Fangs: you cast Hymn at 1:00 / 0:53 (mid-cycle) both pulls, so it was down for Ravenous Feast #2 at 2:15 — the exact moment pull 39 wiped (9 simultaneous deaths) and pull 40 lost two more. In pull 40 it came back at 2:53 and sat available for ~18s through the Caustic Deluge overlap that ended the pull at 3:11. The reference channels it at 0:15 on the opener Deluge, which brings it back precisely for Feast #2. Sszorak kill: you had first-Hymn locked at ~0:50 across six straight prog pulls (4s std dev — fully learned), then pushed it to 1:41 on the kill and lost the 3rd Hymn that belonged in the 4:53→5:37 window where 4 raiders died. Nek'zali proves you can do this: 4/4 Hymns, every one on a damage window. Write the Hymn timings into the plan per boss and treat them like a Feast timer, not a judgment call.
2 — CriticalStop hard-casting Flash Heal into raid damage — that's Prayer of Healing's job
Twin Fangs: 11.8 / 10.6 Flash Heal CPM vs reference 5.1, and zero Prayer of Healing casts in either pull vs the ref's 4.4/min (8.3M healing). Nek'zali: 56 Flash Heals at 79% overheal vs ref's 9; PoH 1.5 CPM vs 7.0. During raid-wide damage in a 4-heal comp with two paladins sniping, single-target hard-casts land on topped bars. The tell is proc share: the reference's Flash-Heal-family casts are 73% Benediction procs; yours are 48-51% — you press MORE flash heals and get FEWER procs, because the globals go into hard casts instead of the PoM/PoH flow that generates procs. PoH also feeds Serendipity (-6s Sanctify). Note the exception: on Sszorak both you and the ref run the single-target build (zero PoH) — there Flash Heal IS the filler, and you under-cast it (5.6 vs 8.7 CPM). Rule of thumb: raid damage → PoH; single-target build fights → FH on cooldown to feed Serenity.
3 — ImportantHoly Word: Serenity cycles 15-31% slower than every reference
Sszorak -21% (37 vs 43 casts), Nek'zali -31% with 32s and 37s idle gaps while charged, Twin Fangs -15/-19%, Vashnik near-match (-7%). With Ultimate Serenity every parked charge is parked AoE healing too. Two causes, both above: fewer fillers = less Serendipity CDR, and holding charges "for the big hit" past the point where the CDR wastes. Serenity overheal 59-70% vs refs' 45-59% says you also fire it late, after bars are topped — cast it INTO Tempest/Feast/Barrage timers, not after them.
4 — ImportantGear: two unenchanted rings, two dead slots, haste where crit should be
Rings: both ilvl 311 rings have no enchant — all 24 reference players enchant both. That's the entire enchant gap; every other slot matches consensus. Slots: Wrist 285 (-23 vs ref avg) and Back 289 (-18) are your two weak pieces; everything else is at or above ref average. Stats: crit 957 (4th-lowest of the 25-player pool, ref avg 1120) vs haste 778 (7th-highest, ref avg 575). Your talent-twin Sodyepi (byte-identical 76-node build, 361k HPS Sszorak parse) runs 1487 crit / 131 haste. Crit > Mastery > Vers > Haste — and your 2pc literally pays out in crit. Shift ~150-200 rating haste→crit via gems/pieces; prefer crit on the wrist/back replacements.
5 — ImportantExternals sit banked while first deaths happen
Guardian Spirit was cast in only 4 of 10 Sszorak pulls — while wipe cascades repeatedly started with tank deaths to Mutilated Gash (pulls 28, 30, 33, 36). It's a 30-second cooldown; it can cover every Mutilate cluster. On Twin Fangs your one GS was reactive — 2 seconds AFTER Brewvoke died to Feast #1. Feast timers are fixed (1:07, 2:15, 3:57...): pre-cast on the Feast target. Same pattern on your Disc kill (Lost Explorers): Pain Suppression banked from ~5:07 while the 5:13 Blast Wave killed Dueche and Klune. The instinct to save externals is costing lives on schedule-known hits.
6 — TuningPrayer of Mending cadence is fight-inconsistent
Reference-grade on Sszorak (7.3/min vs ref 7.0, never capping charges) and Nek'zali (7.45 vs 6.79, max gap 13s) — but on Vashnik it drifted to 12.4s average gaps (28 casts vs ref 49), and with the tier set every missed PoM is a missed auto-Renew: your Renew healing there was 8.6M vs the ref's 22.2M. That one habit on that one fight is worth an estimated 15-25k HPS. The engine works when you focus on it; make the 8-second cadence automatic on every boss.
7 — TuningApotheosis drift and unpaired Halo overheal
Apotheosis gaps run 126-137s on a ~90s CD across fights — usually deliberate window-holding (refs show the same on Sszorak), but it cost 1-2 casts on Nek'zali (7:47 fight, 4 casts) and Vashnik (a free 4th was available). Pull 40 Twin Fangs: Apo #2 held ~60s past ready, then spent at 3:08 as a panic button with 7 already dead — the ref's anchor is ~3:32 on the Ichor→Feast window. On Vashnik, Halo-on-CD ran 76% overheal; the one Halo that met a damage window (2:23) was ~50% more effective — slide each Halo 5-10s to meet the Catalyst/Imbibe timers.
