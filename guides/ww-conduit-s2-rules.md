# Windwalker Monk, Midnight Season 2 (12.1): the Conduit rules as the spec guide states them

Source: a full Season 2 Windwalker spec guide (video transcript, Peak of Serenity theorycrafters credited), distilled 2026-10-22. Spell names are normalized from the auto-transcript (Xuen was "Zuan/Zooin", Windwalker was "Windalker", Shado-Pan was "shadow pan"). Item and enchant names that were garbled beyond recovery are marked (garbled). The transcript was cut off inside the Conduit AoE priority list, so the Shado-Pan section and the tail of the AoE list are absent.

## 1. Season 2 changes

Survivability
- Baseline damage reduction 6% to 10%. Stamina +5% (WW was one of three DPS specs with no stamina bonus in Season 1).
- Dance of the Wind nerfed: 9 stacks (about 91% reduction of the next physical hit, abusable against bleeds and some physical AoE) to 4 stacks (about 46%).
- Vivify and Expel Harm +25%: a wash, every character gained 25% max health this season.
- Touch of Death healing talent 50% to 60%. Vigorous Expulsion 5% to 6%. Silent Sanctuary now scales with healing amps (about 1 to 2% more).
- Touch of Karma and Fortifying Brew gain indirectly from the stamina (both scale with health). No direct defensive buffs.

Burst nerfs (all tied to the Apex talent row)
- Zenith Stomp damage -30%.
- Weapons of Wind (damage during Zenith) 10% to 5%.
- Apex tier 2/3 crit damage bonus 10%/20% to 5%/10%.
- Zenith Stomp now obeys mastery (Combo Strikes): no longer castable back to back at full power. The free Zenith Stomp fired by pressing Zenith counts, so Zenith straight into Zenith Stomp breaks mastery.

Consistent damage buffs
- Auto attacks, Blackout Kick, Tiger Palm, Dual Threat, Spinning Crane Kick, Jade Ignition, Whirling Dragon Punch all buffed. Damage is less reliant on being inside Zenith.

The headline
- Spinning Crane Kick +38%. Temple Training (Conduit) +20%, a direct +20% to Spinning Crane Kick and Fists of Fury on top. Celestial Conduit channel damage -25%.
- With Conduit, Spinning Crane Kick is now the second-strongest ability behind Fists of Fury in both single target and AoE.

## 2. Gearing

Stats
- Mastery highest, haste and crit slightly behind and roughly level with each other, versatility as low as possible.
- Recklessness potion is best in slot: a large bonus to your highest secondary and a cut to your lowest (versatility). Conduit wants mastery as the potion stat. (Shado-Pan: mastery at 2+ targets, haste only in pure single target.)
- Sim upgrades; a best-in-slot piece can still lose to a piece that balances the three stats better.

Tier set (identical for both hero talents)
- 2pc: Fists of Fury strikes one additional time at the start of its channel at 50% effectiveness. Six ticks instead of five, same channel length, and the extra tick triggers Momentum Boost.
- 4pc: each Fists of Fury strike increases the damage of your next Rising Sun Kick by 10% or next Spinning Crane Kick by 20%, stacking to 6. A full channel means Rising Sun Kick +60% or Spinning Crane Kick +120%.
- Value: Shado-Pan ST 3.7% (2pc) + 5.9% (4pc) = 9.6%; Shado-Pan AoE 3.9% + 11.1% = 15%; Conduit ST 3.9% + 4pc (transcribed "6.4% 4%") = 9.6% total; Conduit AoE 5% + 23% = 28%.
- Catalyst now keeps the item's stats. Prioritize haste and mastery pieces. Head, chest and shoulders tier pieces carry good stats; hands and legs carry versatility, so catalyze better-stat pieces for those slots.

Omnium Folio
- Row 1 Rune of Unleashed Fire. Row 2 Self-Mending or Void-Tainted Shell (level). Row 3 locked. Row 4 the stat row: haste or crit, whichever is low (not mastery, the potion already carries it). Row 5 Rune of Overload.

Bonus roll
- Take the Great Vault bonus roll and spend it on the final raid boss every week; in Brewmaster loot spec every drop from that boss is best in slot (weapon, trinket, neck). Often better than taking a vault item.

Crafting
- Wrists first. Then boots or cape depending on whether you will kill Mythic Coiled Altar (it drops a great cape). Embellishment (garbled: "can of weave lighting") on both. Haste and mastery on both; crit if it is low.

Weapons
- Two-hander from the final raid boss (great stats plus a cantrip). One-handers are not close; never farm, craft or dual wield them.

Trinkets
- Active: Voracious Heart of Oolletch (garbled spelling), 90s, main stat, far ahead of everything; times well with 1:30 Conduit. M+ alternative: Freighter Runner's Flask (garbled), crit, 90s. A Season 1 Mythic Puzzle Box beats every Season 2 active trinket until a Normal Oolletch.
- Passive: Zul'jin's Guillotine Technique (garbled) is the best passive, single target and priority target, strong in M+ for boss damage; its 2pc needs dual wield and is skipped. Alternative: Sapling of the Dawnroot (M+, weak in AoE).

Enchants, gems, consumables
- Weapon: haste (do not match the potion stat). Head: Empowered Rune of Avoidance (avoidance early in a tier). Shoulders (garbled: "a midras grace"). Boots (garbled: "linkity"). Chest: Mark of World Soul. Legs: Forest Hunter's armor kit. Rings: Eyes of the Eagle (crit effectiveness), or haste/mastery ring enchants to push the potion threshold.
- Gems: Indecipherable Everong Diamond as the main gem; the rest by stat need (Flawless Deadly Paradox, Flawless Quick Amethyst, Flawless Quick Garnet).
- Flask by stat need. Potion: Recklessness, always. Health potion: Concentrated Silvermoon Health Potion. Weapon oil (garbled: "Lassian Phoenix Oil"). Augment rune when progressing. Food: (garbled: "Harend Celebration"), it carries stamina.

## 3. Conduit: the mechanics

Heart of the Jade Serpent (HotJS)
- Three sources: (1) the Zenith cast, (2) Whirling Dragon Punch or Strike of the Windlord, (3) Unity Within, which fires when Celestial Conduit's channel finishes or is cancelled.
- HotJS reduces the cooldowns of Fists of Fury, Rising Sun Kick, Whirling Dragon Punch and Slicing Winds, and shortens the Fists of Fury channel. The goal inside every window: as many Fists of Fury as possible.
- The WDP (or SotW) HotJS and the Unity Within HotJS do NOT stack. Never overlap them. The Zenith HotJS stacks with both.

The burst window (also the opener)
1. Tiger Palm.
2. Invoke Xuen.
3. Potion, then trinket, then Zenith last (so the Zenith stomp is buffed by both; trinket before potion can push the wrong stat over the potion threshold).
4. Fists of Fury (inside the Zenith HotJS).
5. Rising Sun Kick, then Whirling Dragon Punch: the second HotJS, overlapping Zenith's.
6. Priority list while the WDP HotJS runs: Fists of Fury whenever it is up; Rising Sun Kick to fish for crits that bring Fists back; tier-set consumer per section 4.
7. When the WDP HotJS is about to end or has just ended: Celestial Conduit, cancelled with the highest-priority ability. Unity Within is the third HotJS.
8. Priority inside Unity Within, then Whirling Dragon Punch again: the fourth HotJS.
Four HotJS windows per Xuen window is the target.

The 10-second rule
- Never cast Whirling Dragon Punch when Invoke Xuen has less than 10 seconds of cooldown left. Doing so means WDP is not up when the burst wants it (right before Conduit), which delays Conduit or costs a HotJS window. At 10 seconds or more, a 1 to 2 second delay on WDP inside the burst is fine and all four windows still happen.

Cadence
- Conduit is played at 1:30 or 2:00 depending on talents; pick per boss or dungeon for when burst is needed. The rotation is rigid: delays to the cooldown cycle cost damage.
- Always have a Zenith ready for every Xuen window. On pull you may send two Zeniths; after that only ever send Zenith inside a Xuen window.

The channel
- Always cancel Celestial Conduit. After the 25% channel nerf a full channel is a damage loss. Press your next ability as soon as the GCD allows; the cancel grants Unity Within and you go straight back into the rotation. Use cancel macros.
- Fists of Fury is the trap: never spam Fists before the Conduit GCD has finished. An early Fists goes out WITHOUT Unity Within and wastes 2 to 3 seconds of the window.

Tigereye Brew
- With Conduit you naturally overstack Tigereye Brew because of the long cooldowns. Ignore the stacks; play the cooldown cycle.

## 4. Tier-set consumer after a full Fists of Fury

Single target
1. Rushing Wind Kick, if available.
2. Spinning Crane Kick with a Dance of Chi-Ji proc.
3. Rising Sun Kick, only during Bloodlust or when it enables Whirling Dragon Punch (keeping the HotJS chain alive).
4. Baseline Spinning Crane Kick.
A naked Rising Sun Kick that does not enable WDP sits below Spinning Crane Kick.

Two or more targets
- Spinning Crane Kick. Only Spinning Crane Kick, baseline or with Dance of Chi-Ji. Rushing Wind Kick is still worth casting for its Fists cooldown reduction, but never as the 4pc consumer.

## 5. Talent builds

Single target
- Two points in Apex only (no Zenith Stomp). Drinking Horn Cover (longer Zenith, synergy with Weapons of Wind). Spiritual Focus is a possible later swap.

AoE / Mythic+
- Full Apex (Zenith Stomp stays strong at 2+ targets).
- Drinking Horn Cover or Spiritual Focus: preference.
- Energy Burst or Inner Peace: one of them is mandatory in every dungeon.
- High-AoE dungeons: take both Inner Peace and Energy Burst.
- Priority-damage dungeons, or a group light on priority damage: Martial Agility instead of one of those two.
- Obsidian Spiral is not run.

## 6. Priority lists (Peak of Serenity default list)

Single target (15 steps)
1. Whirling Dragon Punch, if the next Invoke Xuen is more than 10 seconds away.
2. Celestial Conduit, if no HotJS from WDP or SotW is active.
3. Zenith Stomp if low on chi or Zenith is almost over (not talented in single target).
4. Fists of Fury if HotJS has less than 1 second left (it still gets the shortened channel).
5. Tiger Palm to avoid capping energy outside Zenith or Bloodlust, or when chi is short for Fists of Fury.
6. Fists of Fury (inside or outside HotJS, the top chi spender).
7. Rushing Wind Kick (the single-target 4pc consumer).
8. Spinning Crane Kick with the 4pc buff, Dance of Chi-Ji included.
9. Rising Sun Kick.
10. Blackout Kick with Combo Breaker (or during Zenith with Obsidian Spiral, not run). Combo Breaker is the only route to Rushing Wind Kick, so it sits below RWK but is never let overcap.
11. Spinning Crane Kick during Zenith with more than 4 chi or Dance of Chi-Ji.
12. Tiger Palm with less than 2 chi.
13. Spinning Crane Kick to dump chi.
14. Tiger Palm without overcapping chi.
15. Blackout Kick.
Combo Breaker and Dance of Chi-Ji both stack to 2; do not let either reach a wasted third proc, use them a little earlier than the list says if they are about to cap, unless Fists spam inside HotJS is on offer, which wins.

Two or more targets (21 steps; the transcript ends inside step 11)
1. Fists of Fury if HotJS is about to end.
2. Whirling Dragon Punch if Xuen's cooldown is longer than 10 seconds.
3. Strike of the Windlord (not taken).
4. Zenith Stomp if low on chi or Zenith is almost over: use it as the chi filler in place of Tiger Palm, and get every stomp out before Zenith ends.
5. Tiger Palm when chi is short for Fists of Fury.
6. Fists of Fury.
7. Spinning Crane Kick after every Fists of Fury and before the next one (the 4pc consumer at 2+ targets).
8. Tiger Palm to avoid energy capping outside Zenith.
9. Rising Sun Kick to enable Whirling Dragon Punch.
10. Rushing Wind Kick without the 4pc buff up (still cast for its Fists cooldown reduction).
11. Rising Sun Kick ... (transcript cut off here).

## 7. Mastery (Combo Strikes)
- Never press the same damaging ability twice in a row. Zenith Stomp counts, and the free stomp from the Zenith press counts as a Zenith Stomp cast.

## 8. What the guide says you should be thinking about between bursts
- Two questions, constantly: when can I next cast Whirling Dragon Punch (for a HotJS window that fits Fists of Fury), and what am I spending the 4pc stacks on. Chase Rising Sun Kick crits only when they can bring Fists of Fury back inside a HotJS window.
