# Elemental Shaman, Midnight Season 2 (12.1.5): the Farseer rotation guide

The readable version of guides/ele-s2-rules.md, distilled 2026-10-30 from eighteen Season 2 YouTube guides plus Method (12.1.5, 14 Oct), Maxroll and Murlok's top 50, corrected against two verifiers. The heavy book holds every quote, timestamp, disagreement and the 78 audit rules. This one is for playing. Where the guides split, the call is stated and section 12 says who said what.

## 1. Season 2 changes

- Farseer replaced Stormbringer for all content. Tempest and the mastery overloads were nerfed going into 12.1, Ancestors' Chain Lightning now hits 5 targets, and Farseer is "much more forgiving when you make rotational mistakes" (Mac). The top 50 run it 50 of 50.
- Power moved out of Ascendance into the baseline kit. You are less cooldown-reliant than in Season 1 and your damage outside Ascendance is no longer "losing to tanks" (BOND).
- Aftershock is out. The tier set gives free spenders, so Tectonic Collapse takes its place.
- Power of the Maelstrom is passive now: a Lightning Bolt or Chain Lightning has a 15 percent chance to buff your next Lava Burst by 20 percent. Nothing to manage.
- Primordial Wave and Icefury are gone. Purging Flames brings back the Lava Burst that hits every Flame Shocked target.
- Earthquake can be cast on your target or at a location, same damage (Petko). Method prefers targeted. If you play targeted, check what you have selected before you press it.
- Ancestral Swiftness replaces Nature's Swiftness for Farseer: the next cast is instant, deals 10 percent more and summons an Ancestor for 8 seconds.
- Lava Burst holds 3 charges with Farseer.
- The tier set is "probably the biggest thing in 12.1 that actually changes how the spec feels to play" (Mac) and "the only min-max part" (Petko). Section 7.
- 12.1.5 changed the single-target opener (section 5). No guide names any other 12.1.5 change for Elemental.

## 2. Gearing

Stats
- Mastery first, to about 1100 to 1200 rating. The overload part of mastery caps at 100 percent, around 1200 rating, and the return falls off a cliff past it (Realm Gaming). The top 50 sit at mastery 1094, crit 955, haste 762.
- Then crit at or above haste. BOND plays crit equals haste, Petko leans haste, the live top 50 lean crit (26 against 21 percent).
- Versatility last. Sim before you craft.

Tier set
- 2pc: Earth Shock, Elemental Blast and Earthquake deal 25 percent more. Passive.
- 4pc: when Stormkeeper's charges are spent or Ascendance fades, you gain Flowing Elements (2 stacks per fade, 4 if both fade together, cap 4, so a fade landing on 3 or 4 held stacks wastes them). Each Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning or Lava Burst eats a stack, deals 25 percent more, and grants Overcharge, which makes your next Elemental Blast or Earthquake free. Overcharge does not stack. Section 7 is how you play it.
- Ula'tek's chest keeps its crit cantrip when you catalyze it into tier (Money).

Embellishments and crafts
- Two embellishments. Arcanoweave Lining fills 92 of 100 top-50 slots. BOND and Petko run the Hunter's Ritual Stone on a crafted off-hand, about 1 percent ahead per BOND, plus Arcanoweave on bracers or boots. Nobody crafts a weapon.
- Weapon: the last raid boss's dagger (Jan'thrazet, the Soul Fang) is best in slot and 47 of 50 carry it. Non-raiders craft a two-hander.

Trinkets
- One on-use plus one passive (Petko), or two passives (Money, "our current profile is not as chained to Ascendance as it used to be").
- The top 50: Twisted Horror's Tendril 46, Gebbo's Bottomless Bag 33 (Petko's best in slot, from the third raid boss), Vile Vial of Volatile Venom 18. BOND: do not take the last boss's trinket.

Enchants, gems, consumables
- Head Empowered Rune of Avoidance. Shoulders Amirdrassil's Grace. Chest Mark of the Worldsoul. Legs Sunfire Silk Spellthread. Feet Lynx's Dexterity. Rings Eyes of the Eagle, both. Weapon Rite of the Hash'ey (50 of 50, BOND says sim it). The top 50 show no cloak or bracer enchant.
- Gems: a Powerful Eversong Diamond (or the Indecipherable one) plus one gem of each colour for its effect: Flawless Deadly Amethyst, Quick Garnet, Masterful Garnet, Deadly Peridot, Deadly Lapis.
- Flask of the Magisters or of the Blood Knights, by sim. Hearty Harandar Celebration or Royal Roast. Light's Potential is the combat potion, on the Ascendance macro, held only when a boss pull falls inside its cooldown (Money). Concentrated Silvermoon Health Potion. Void-Touched Augment Rune. Flametongue Weapon is your imbue, Thalassian Phoenix Oil only if it is not talented.
- Before the first pull: Lightning Shield, Flametongue Weapon, Earth Shield on yourself if talented (38 of 50, and its 9 charges run out, so reapply it), Skyfury on the group.

The Ascendance macro

Slot 13 is the top trinket, 14 the bottom. Everything on it is off the global cooldown, so it fires on the press. Keep a separate Ancestral Swiftness key for the casts between Ascendances.

## 3. Farseer: the mechanics

Maelstrom
- Elemental Blast costs 90. Earthquake costs 60. Builders: Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning (per target hit), Lava Burst, Flame Shock ticks, overloads. Ancestors generate none.
- The cap: no guide names it. BOND treats 123 as overcapping with Swelling Maelstrom. Get a bar for it, "overcapping is a huge waste of damage".

Master of the Elements
- A Lava Burst cast makes your next spell deal 15 percent more. Almost anything consumes it, Flame Shock and Voltaic Blaze included. The whole single-target game is putting it on Elemental Blast.

Lava Burst and Lava Surge
- 3 charges, about 8 seconds each. Flame Shock ticks proc Lava Surge: a charge back and the next Lava Burst instant. Lava Burst always crits on a Flame Shocked target.

Flame Shock, Voltaic Blaze, Purging Flames
- Voltaic Blaze replaces Flame Shock on your bars and applies it to everything it hits. About 8 seconds of cooldown after Crackling Fury and Flames of the Cauldron.
- Every Voltaic Blaze grants Purging Flames: your next Lava Burst also fires at 5 more Flame Shocked targets at 80 percent, and Lava Burst deals 10 percent more. Pressing Voltaic Blaze again with Purging Flames unspent overwrites it.
- Flame Shock lasts 18 seconds. Refresh at 6 seconds or less. A refresh at 7 seconds resets it to 18 and throws the rest away. A Flame Shock applied inside Ascendance lasts 36, so its window is about 10. Any Flame Shocked target takes 10 percent more from you.

Ancestors (Call of the Ancestors)
- Stormkeeper and Ancestral Swiftness each summon one. Lightning Bolt, Lava Burst, Flame Shock, Voltaic Blaze and Chain Lightning each have an 8 percent chance to summon one.
- An Ancestor copies you: Lava Burst after your single-target spell, Chain Lightning (5 targets) after your AoE spell. When you cast Voltaic Blaze they fire one Lava Burst per Flame Shocked mob.
- When an Ancestor departs it casts Elemental Blast (Final Calling), which gives you the Elemental Blast stat buffs. That is why you stop casting Elemental Blast in AoE after the first one.
- Every Ancestor summoned takes 3 seconds off Stormkeeper's cooldown.

The cooldowns
- Stormkeeper: 60 seconds, a cast time, 2 charges. The next 2 Lightning Bolts (150 percent more) or Chain Lightnings (an overload on every target) are instant. With the Ancestor reduction it runs at about 40 to 45 seconds in practice.
- Ancestral Swiftness: 30 seconds, off the global cooldown. Next cast instant and 10 percent stronger, one Ancestor.
- Ascendance: 15 seconds, 2 minutes with First Ascendant (38 of 50) or 3 with Preeminence (the other 12, Money: it "will see play"). Overload damage up 30 percent and every mastery spell overloads once more. It also Flame Shocks up to 6 targets at 50 percent, and the first one lasts 36 seconds.
- Elemental Blast gives 7 percent crit or haste, or 14 percent mastery, for 10 seconds, one at random. Inside Ascendance the extra overloads can hand you all three from one cast.

## 4. The three cooldowns: order and holds

The order, when they go together: Stormkeeper, then Ancestral Swiftness, then Ascendance.
- Stormkeeper first because Ancestral Swiftness's Ancestor takes 3 seconds off a Stormkeeper that is on cooldown and nothing off one that is sitting ready. Two Swiftness casts per Stormkeeper cycle is 6 seconds guaranteed. And Stormkeeper is a setup cast, you never spend an Ascendance global on it.
- Never start a Stormkeeper cast inside Ascendance. If it comes back during the window it waits for the fade, and Ancestral Swiftness still goes on cooldown meanwhile.

Stormkeeper
- On cooldown. Hold it for Ascendance up to about 10 seconds (Method, BOND, Mac, Money all give 10). Past 10 the hold costs you a cast over the key, and the lost cast is the mistake. In keys Mac lets it "drift a bit", 10 seconds late is "not necessarily a mistake anymore".
- Pre-cast it before every pull. On an add fight, pre-cast it before the adds spawn.
- On a boss, spend both charges before Ascendance (section 5). On a trash pull, together with Ascendance (the Stormkeeper cast finished before Ascendance lands, nothing spent between) or before it, both are fine.
- When it comes back and Ascendance is down, cast it alone and spend both charges.

Ancestral Swiftness
- On cooldown, every 30 seconds. "I see people sitting on this ability for way too long and it is a massive damage loss" (Mac).
- The one hold: when Stormkeeper is 10 seconds or less from ready, or Ascendance is about that close, hold it and send the three together.
- What the instant goes on: on 2 to 3 targets, the Lava Burst that consumes Purging Flames. On 4 or more, the one Elemental Blast at the start of the pull. On a boss, the Elemental Blast that opens Ascendance. Chain Lightning is fine, it has a cast time. Never Earth Shock (Method). Never a Lava Surge Lava Burst, already instant, which wastes half the buff. Keep a Lava Burst charge for it (Method).

Ascendance
- On cooldown. "More casts equals more damage. Ascendance is our biggest button" (Mac). A key with one Ascendance fewer than it could have had is the failure.
- The one override: will the target survive it. Send it on a pack of three, on a pack at half health or above, on a mini boss. Never into a pack that is already melting. Never wait for the perfect pull. Planned high-key routes are the exception.
- Have about 50 Maelstrom when you press it mid-fight or on one target, so the first cast inside is Elemental Blast and its buffs ride the window (Mac). On a 4+ pull BOND wants "slightly above 10" from the gather, the pull builds the rest. On a raid pull you have none, so just send it.
- Potion, on-use trinket and Bloodlust fire with it (the macro).
- Money's limit for syncing: hold Ascendance up to about 15 seconds, or Stormkeeper up to about 10, to pair them. He is the only guide with a key figure for Ascendance. The rule that bites is the lost cast.

Voltaic Blaze inside Ascendance: BOND casts it (Purging Flames is a lot of damage, and the Ancestors fire a Lava Burst per Flame Shocked mob). Mac's August guide says no, your Ancestors would cast Lava Burst instead of Chain Lightning. Unresolved. BOND is the higher-rated player.

## 5. The opener

A boss (Mac, 28 Oct, from Fonnie's log, a GG log and BOND's add phase)
1. Stormkeeper at 1.5 to 2 seconds on the pull timer (BOND hard casts his at about 1 second).
2. Lightning Bolt.
3. Lightning Bolt. Both Stormkeeper charges spent before Ascendance, so the tier set's free spenders land inside the window.
4. Ascendance with the macro (Ancestral Swiftness, potion, trinkets). Bloodlust here if it is yours.
5. Lava Burst, Elemental Blast.
6. Lava Burst, Elemental Blast.
7. Lava Burst, Elemental Blast.
8. The single-target loop.
- On a boss with a damage-taken phase, Flame Shock it before the pull for the 10 percent (BOND).
- No pre-pull Lava Burst this season. It makes you pre-cast Stormkeeper at 4 seconds and leaves "an ancestor out that's not doing anything" for two seconds. Method's page still prints the old order (Stormkeeper at 3 seconds, Lava Burst at 1.5, cooldowns, Lightning Bolt), BOND's default fades Stormkeeper with Ascendance for 4 stacks and calls spending it first "a very small gain" in single target. Either way is fine. Mac's is the newest.
- Elemental Blast goes first inside Ascendance: it is the hardest hit, and Ascendance's extra overloads can grant all three of its buffs from the first cast, 100 percent buff uptime through the window. On a pull from zero Maelstrom you cannot, so do not force it.

A Mythic+ pull (BOND)
1. Target the priority mob while the tank gathers. On a big pull, one or two Chain Lightnings during the gather so you have Maelstrom.
2. Voltaic Blaze. Before Ascendance every time, so its Lava Burst is ready inside the window (BOND puts it before every cooldown, Method after Stormkeeper and Ancestral Swiftness).
3. Stormkeeper.
4. Ascendance with the macro (Ancestral Swiftness fires with it). Bloodlust on the big pull.
5. 2 to 3 targets: the Ancestral Swiftness instant goes on Lava Burst, which consumes Purging Flames. 4 or more: it goes on one Elemental Blast for its buffs. "You only have to do this one time at the beginning of your pulls", your Ancestors maintain the buffs after that.
6. The two Stormkeeper Chain Lightnings.
7. Then the AoE priority: Lava Burst on the Lava Surge proc under Purging Flames, Earthquake, Chain Lightning.

## 6. Priority lists

Method 12.1.5, single target, M+ Build (verbatim)
1. Stormkeeper. May be held for damage amps or AoE, but ideally not more than 10 seconds.
2. Ancestral Swiftness.
3. Ascendance, with trinket, potion, and Bloodlust.
4. Refresh Voltaic Blaze if Fire Elemental has under 3 seconds left.
5. Refresh Voltaic Blaze with under 6 seconds left.
6. Elemental Blast when within 15 Maelstrom of cap.
7. Lava Burst when there is no Master of the Elements buff.
8. Elemental Blast with Master of the Elements.
9. Lightning Bolt.

The single-target loop in one breath (Mac): if you are within about 15 Maelstrom of capping, Elemental Blast, that beats everything. Otherwise, do you have Master of the Elements? Yes: spend it, ideally on Elemental Blast. No: Lava Burst to get it. Lightning Bolt when neither is available. Flame Shock upkeep sits under all of it, refreshed in the pandemic window.
- Never two Lava Bursts in a row.
- Hold a Lava Burst charge for the Elemental Blast that is about to be affordable. Never hit zero charges, so a Lava Surge proc does not overcap you. Hold two charges as Stormkeeper or Ascendance is about to end, for the two free spenders.
- If Elemental Blast would overcap you, Master of the Elements on a Lightning Bolt is fine (BOND). Never stall the rotation to force the pairing.
- In keys, Voltaic Blaze on one target is your Flame Shock refresh (Method, Mac, Rampage). Press it at 6 seconds or less, or when Fire Elemental has under 3 seconds left. Mac adds: with Master of the Elements down. Method's list has no such condition, so a refresh that eats the buff is a note, not a mistake. Petko, Javier and Kephas press it on cooldown, the written sources do not.

Method 12.1.5, AoE, M+ Build (verbatim)
- 2 targets: the single-target list, but Voltaic Blaze on cooldown, and Chain Lightning instead of Lightning Bolt.
- 3 to 5 targets: Stormkeeper. Ancestral Swiftness. Voltaic Blaze on cooldown. Ascendance with trinket, potion and Bloodlust. Lava Burst with Purging Flames, ideally on a Lava Surge proc. Earthquake when Ancestors are up. Elemental Blast when no Ancestors are up. Chain Lightning.
- 5 or more: the 3 to 5 list, but always Earthquake, regardless of Ancestors.

The table the videos play
- 1 target: the loop above. Lightning Bolt fills.
- 2 targets: Elemental Blast is the spender, never Earthquake. Chain Lightning fills (Mac's August guide alone kept Lightning Bolt). Voltaic Blaze on cooldown (Method, BOND, Petko) or as the refresh (Mac's September guide keeps the single-target list at 2 to 3). Hard-cast Lava Burst freely, back to back is fine, "you don't play Master of the Elements" here (Petko).
- 3 targets: Chain Lightning fills. Earthquake if you have an Ancestor out, Elemental Blast if you have none. About 1.5 percent between them, BOND says Elemental Blast at 3 is "perfectly fine". Lava Burst is contested: BOND and Petko instant-only, Mac's September guide keeps the weave with hard casts.
- 4 targets: Earthquake for Mac, BOND, Realm Gaming, Money and Kephas. Method's 3 to 5 list allows Elemental Blast with no Ancestors up, Petko one when you hold no Elemental Blast buff. Either is defensible at exactly 4. Voltaic Blaze on cooldown, Lava Burst only instant, only to consume Purging Flames.
- 5 or more: Earthquake is the spender, always. One Elemental Blast at the pull start for its buffs, then none unless the pack is spread or moving. Voltaic Blaze on cooldown, "the backbone of the entire spec". Lava Burst only when instant and only to consume Purging Flames. Chain Lightning fills.
- A tank that kites or a pack that will walk out of the Earthquake: Elemental Blast stays the better spender.

The Purging Flames sequence at 4 or more targets (Mac)
1. Voltaic Blaze. Purging Flames is up for the 8 seconds until it comes back.
2. Hold your Lava Surge proc. "Just because you have the Lava Surge proc, doesn't mean you need to immediately send the Lava Burst."
3. Chain Lightning until you can afford Earthquake.
4. The Lava Burst, instant, hitting everything.
5. Earthquake straight after, so Master of the Elements lands on the spender and not on a Chain Lightning.
6. Voltaic Blaze again the moment it is back, with Purging Flames spent.
- Never let Purging Flames go unspent into the next Voltaic Blaze. The one hard cast allowed in AoE is the Lava Burst that clears it when Voltaic Blaze is about to come back and no proc came. With Voltaic Blaze still seconds away, a hard cast is the same mistake as one without Purging Flames.
- Never break Chain Lightning just to put Master of the Elements on an Earthquake. In AoE the buff only matters around the Purging Flames Lava Burst.

## 7. The tier set window

When Stormkeeper's charges are spent, and again when Ascendance fades, Flowing Elements lands. 2 stacks each, 4 if they fade together.
1. As the fade approaches, dump your Maelstrom. Free spenders cost nothing, so you want room to keep generating.
2. Builder (Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning or Lava Burst): eats a stack, hits 25 percent harder, grants Overcharge.
3. Spender (Elemental Blast single target, Earthquake AoE): free.
4. Builder.
5. Spender.
Repeat until the stacks are gone.
- Two builders in a row throw a free spender away, Overcharge stacks to 1. Mac calls it the mistake he sees most. "You will waste 1 stack of Overcharge, so be careful with that" (BOND).
- Spend the stacks before the next fade. Flowing Elements caps at 4, a fade that lands on 3 or 4 held stacks wastes them: "never ever overcap on the tier set" (Petko).
- Lava Burst is the builder you want, its Master of the Elements rides onto the free spender. But do not wait for a charge: "If lava burst isn't up, don't wait and you definitely don't overwrite a tier set buff trying to wait for a charge to come back."
- Near the cap inside the window, drop a paid spender between the free ones. Back-to-back spenders are fine, back-to-back builders are not, and a builder at the cap is wrong inside the window as much as outside it.
- The munch at the fade: a hard-cast Lightning Bolt started with less than a second of Ascendance left lands after the fade, eats a stack at once, and a Lava Burst right behind it wastes an Overcharge. Two or three seconds before the fade, decide which cast lands inside and which lands after.
- The last Overcharge can be held and buffed by a Lava Burst later.
- Track Flowing Elements and Overcharge in the Cooldown Manager (/cdm, buffs, drag them from "not displayed"). BOND adds a sound alert on Flowing Elements.
- On a boss, Mac spends Stormkeeper before Ascendance so its two free spenders are Lava Weaved inside the window and the charges recover before Ascendance fades. On trash, BOND fades them together for 4 stacks at once. Both pass.

## 8. Flame Shock and Voltaic Blaze, the rules

- Flame Shock on everything you want to Lava Burst: it is the crit and the Lava Surge source. In keys Voltaic Blaze does the applying. Keep it on all your targets (Realm Gaming).
- Refresh at 6 seconds or less (about 10 on the 36-second one Ascendance applies), never earlier, except when Fire Elemental has under 3 seconds left (Method).
- Voltaic Blaze on cooldown at 4 or more targets. At 2 to 3, Method, BOND and Petko say on cooldown and Mac's September guide keeps it as the Flame Shock refresh. At 1, the refresh only (Method, Mac, Rampage), though Petko, Javier and Kephas press it on cooldown.
- A Lava Burst follows every Voltaic Blaze before the next one. Instant at 4 or more, hard cast at 1 or 2, contested at 3.
- Voltaic Blaze goes before Ascendance on every pull.

## 10. Mythic+ utility and defensives

Staying alive
- "Just live. If you are dead, your overall damage suffers tremendously" (BOND).
- Astral Shift is the one true defensive: 40 percent on 1.5 minutes. Learn the hits and press it before them.
- Earth Elemental with Primordial Bond: 15 percent more maximum health while it is out, no taunt. Your second defensive.
- Earth Shield on yourself if talented. Healing Surge exists. The health potion does not share a cooldown with Light's Potential.
- Be the kicker. Windveil gives you 15 percent spell damage reduction for 12 seconds after your Wind Shear lands, "it's not enough to just assume someone else will get the kick" (Mac). Wind Shear is 12 seconds.

Movement
- Spiritwalker's Grace before the movement starts, not after. "When in doubt, just send it." On a fight you do not know, pair it with Ascendance.
- Move between globals and on instants: the Stormkeeper bolts, Ancestral Swiftness, Lava Surge procs. Gust of Wind to reposition.

Utility
- Capacitor Totem on cooldown on trash (Realm Gaming). It lands 2 seconds after the press, so it struggles with 1.2 second casts.
- Skyfury on yourself and the group. Heroism on the opening Ascendance of the big pull or on the boss, agreed with the party.
- Purge magic shields and buffs. Cleanse Spirit the curses. Poison Cleansing Totem the poisons.

The dungeon map (Money, who hedges almost every line)
- Ruby Life Pools: being ready to Purge the Flame Lashers' shield when they drop to low health and channel "can help".
- Murder Row: Capacitor Totem the Street Sneaks' Heartstop Poison (1.2 seconds, not kickable), Poison Cleansing Totem "can cover a missed stop". Cleanse Spirit the Corrupted Warlocks' Curse of Doom after boss two, "make everyone's life easier".
- Blinding Vale: Poison Cleansing Totem on the Matriarch's AoE poison near the end, "a single global to mitigate a lot of damage".
- King's Rest: Purge the minions of Zul before they fear, or take Tremor Totem and run into the fears. King Rahu'ai's 30 percent damage reduction in the gauntlet after boss one is "worth purging".
- Temple of Sethraliss: Poison Cleansing Totem for Cytotoxin after the bridge. Purge Accumulate Charge off the Agitated Nimbus and they never cast Release Charge, the one line he writes as a must.
- Den of Nalorakk: kill the Starvation Effigy, targeted curse dispels "can help" at high stacks. Poison Cleansing Totem "can trivialize" the Hoardmonger's mushroom soaks.

## 11. What to be thinking about between windows

- Three timers, always: when is Ancestral Swiftness back (30 seconds), when is Stormkeeper back (about 45), when is Ascendance back (2 minutes, 3 with Preeminence). Pair them when they are within 10 seconds, send them when they are not.
- How much Maelstrom do I have, and what is the spender for this target count. You never sit at the cap, and you walk into a mid-fight Ascendance at about 50.
- Where is Purging Flames. Voltaic Blaze just went out: is my Lava Surge proc in hand, is my Maelstrom ready for the Earthquake behind it.
- How many Lava Burst charges do I have. One in the chamber always, two when a fade is coming, one spare when Ancestral Swiftness is about to come up.
- When is the next fade, and which cast lands after it. How many stacks am I still holding. The tier set is where good and great Elementals separate.
- Will this pack live through Ascendance. If yes, press it.

## 12. Where the guides split

- Stormkeeper cast before Ascendance (Mac, Realm Gaming, Kephas, Saltii, Rampage) or with it (BOND in AoE for 4 stacks). Both charges spent before: Mac's two newest videos and BOND's damage-phase opener only, against Method's opener, BOND's default, Realm Gaming and Mac's August opener. Call: cast it before, on a boss spend both before, either on trash.
- Master of the Elements in the keys build: taken by the top 50 and in Mac's and BOND's later play, left out of BOND's launch build and Money's. Call: if talented, weave.
- Earthquake at 3 (BOND, Money, Kephas, Method by Ancestors) or 4 (Mac, Realm Gaming, Rampage), and at exactly 4 Method and Petko allow a conditional Elemental Blast. Call: never at 2, the Ancestor rule at 3, either at 4, always from 5.
- Chain Lightning at 2 (Method, BOND, Petko, Mac's September guide) or 3 (Mac's August guide, Rampage). Call: 2, the newer Mac wins.
- Pre-pull Lava Burst: Method and BOND's launch opener yes, Mac's 12.1.5 opener no. Call: no, and no fault either way.
- Hard-cast Lava Burst in AoE: never at 4 or more (Petko, BOND, Mac) except to clear Purging Flames before Voltaic Blaze returns. Freely at 2. At 3, BOND and Petko instant-only, Mac's September guide hard casts. Call: contested at 3.
- Voltaic Blaze on one target: refresh only (Method, Mac, Rampage) or on cooldown (Petko, Javier, Kephas). Call: refresh only. At 2 to 3: on cooldown (Method, BOND, Petko) or the refresh (Mac's September guide). Call: contested.
- Master of the Elements eaten by a Voltaic Blaze refresh: a loss for Mac, unconditioned in Method's list. Call: a note.
