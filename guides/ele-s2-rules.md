# Elemental Shaman, Midnight Season 2 (12.1 / 12.1.5): the rules as the guides state them

Compiled 2026-10-30 for grading a Farseer Elemental Shaman Mythic+ log in wcl-analyzer. Sources: three written guides (Method, Maxroll, Murlok's top 50) and eighteen YouTube videos found by eight searches over everything uploaded since 2026-06-01 (51 unique hits, selection.md in the session scratchpad records every one and why it is in or out). Fourteen videos were read from YouTube's captions, seven of those were also transcribed with Whisper while YouTube rate-limited the caption endpoint, and two were run through Whisper because YouTube had no captions and turned out to hold no speech. Two more were transcribed and then thrown out because they are about "World of Warcraft Forever", a vanilla-style game, not Midnight.

Names are normalized from the auto-captions and from Whisper: "Volcanic Blaze", "Voltic Blast", "Voltaire Blaze" and "VB" are Voltaic Blaze. "Ellie Blast", "Ele Blast" and "EB" are Elemental Blast. "Ascendant", "ascendants" and "a sentence" are Ascendance. "Storm Keeper" and "SK" are Stormkeeper. "mail storm" is Maelstrom. "Ancestral Guidance" in Mac's August guide is Ancestral Swiftness (the slot and the same author's later videos settle it). "grandparents", "the guys" and "ancestors" are the Call of the Ancestors summons. "Kick DR" and "Windvil" are Windveil, the class talent that gives 15 percent spell damage reduction for 12 seconds after a Wind Shear interrupt (Murlok: 47 of 50 take it). "the meatball barrage" is Mac's name for the Lava Burst cast under Purging Flames. "Flowing Elements" and "Overcharge" are the two buffs of the tier 4-piece, named by BOND, Petko and Money.

A figure marked [log] is to be measured from the logs before it is graded, because no source names it. A cut-off marked (grading tolerance) is this rulebook's choice where the sources give only words. A rule marked "count" or "report" goes into the report and is not a per-press fault.

## 2. What changed for Season 2 / 12.1 / 12.1.5

- Farseer replaced Stormbringer for all content. Mac (O2lXhuipom0, 00:33): "the expected meta for pretty much everything is Farseer over Stormbringer, which is basically the exact opposite of where we started Midnight." Why (00:42): "Both Tempest and our mastery overloads took a big nerf moving into 12.1 and that naturally hurt Stormbringer a lot". (01:09): "Ancestors Chain Lightning now hits five targets instead of three". BOND (01:18): "forget about Stormbringer. We are Farseer for all content." Petko (00:50): "Do not play Stormbringer." Murlok: 50 of 50.
- Power moved out of Ascendance into the baseline. BOND (00:48): "they removed power from our Ascendance, and they gave it to us as damage outside of Ascendance." Mac (O2lXhuipom0, 02:02): "The 12.1 tuning did push more of our damage into our baseline kit." Maxroll's changelog: Ascendance's overload bonus "30% (was 75%)" (its summary still says 150 percent, a contradiction on its own page).
- Aftershock is out, Tectonic Collapse is in, because the tier set gives free spenders. Mac (O2lXhuipom0, 03:39). BOND (01:23): "Aftershock is out. No more aftershock. We're running Tectonic Collapse."
- Power of the Maelstrom was reworked: Lightning Bolt or Chain Lightning has "a 15% chance to empower your next lava burst by 20%" (Mac, O2lXhuipom0, 04:03). Nothing to manage.
- Primordial Wave is removed (Maxroll). Icefury is gone (Money, A6w0oYK6GnI, 01:10). Purging Flames brings back the Lava Burst that hits every Flame Shocked target (Petko, 09:40).
- Earthquake now lands on the target rather than at the cursor (Realm Gaming, 07:44). Method prefers targeted Earthquake to cursor placement.
- Ancestral Swiftness replaced Nature's Swiftness for Farseer: the next cast is instant, deals 10 percent more and summons an Ancestor for 8 seconds (Realm Gaming 14:09, Mac CYwhgHdBAAM 04:56).
- Lava Burst holds 3 charges with Farseer (Mac zVjen8YoWJQ 00:44, Realm Gaming 20:46).
- The Season 2 tier set (section 11) is "the biggest thing in 12.1 that actually changes how the spec feels to play" (Mac, O2lXhuipom0, 09:39) and "the only min-max part" (Petko, 07:50).
- 12.1.5 (released before 14 Oct 2026): Method and Maxroll are updated to it. The only video that names it is Mac's 28 Oct opener, which changes the single-target opener from last season's (section 5). No source lists 12.1.5 tuning for Elemental.

## 4. The mechanics

- Maelstrom: Elemental Blast costs 90 (Realm Gaming 06:26, BOND iI2O02l_tE4 12:54), Earthquake 60 (Javier 04:16). The cap is not named by any source. BOND treats 123 as overcapping and casts a spender at 117 "trying not to overcap" (iI2O02l_tE4 05:47, 06:03). Swelling Maelstrom raises the cap and 47 of 50 take it. [log] Read the cap from the resource events before grading overcap.
- Maelstrom is generated by Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning (per target hit), Lava Burst, Flame Shock ticks and overloads (Realm Gaming 03:28, 16:58, 17:12). Ancestors generate none (15:29).
- Master of the Elements: a Lava Burst cast makes the next spell deal 15 percent more (Mac zVjen8YoWJQ 00:06, gUDeNbgw34I 08:43). Almost any spell consumes it, Flame Shock and Voltaic Blaze included (gUDeNbgw34I 01:51).
- Lava Burst: 3 charges with Farseer, about 8 seconds a charge (zVjen8YoWJQ 00:44). Lava Surge: Flame Shock ticks reset a charge and make the next Lava Burst instant (Realm Gaming 02:26). Lava Burst always crits on a Flame Shocked target (Realm Gaming 04:31).
- Flame Shock: 18 seconds, pandemic window 6 seconds or less, a refresh at 7 seconds resets to 18 and loses the remainder (Realm Gaming 21:54). Ascendance applies Flame Shock at 50 percent to up to 6 targets and makes the first one last 36 seconds (Realm Gaming 08:14, 10:38). Any Flame Shocked target takes 10 percent more damage from you (BOND iI2O02l_tE4 11:32).
- Voltaic Blaze replaces Flame Shock on the bars and applies it to the targets it hits (Realm Gaming 05:40). Its cooldown is about 8 seconds (Mac zVjen8YoWJQ 04:46) after Crackling Fury and Flames of the Cauldron take "a total of 7 and 1/2 seconds off" (gUDeNbgw34I 04:13). It grants Purging Flames.
- Purging Flames: the next Lava Burst also fires at 5 additional Flame Shocked targets at 80 percent effectiveness with reduced Maelstrom generation, and Lava Burst deals 10 percent more (Realm Gaming's tooltip read, 23:09). Casting Voltaic Blaze again with Purging Flames unspent overwrites it (Mac zVjen8YoWJQ 06:08).
- Stormkeeper: 60 second cooldown, cast time, 2 charges, the next 2 Lightning Bolts (150 percent more damage) or Chain Lightnings (an overload on every target) are instant (Realm Gaming 15:52). Every Ancestor summoned takes 3 seconds off its cooldown (Mac gUDeNbgw34I 05:56, BOND 18:43), so its effective cooldown is "40 to 45 seconds" (Mac CYwhgHdBAAM 03:08, Money 7HOSKorzVpw 06:09 "about 45 seconds").
- Ancestral Swiftness: 30 second cooldown, off the global cooldown, the next cast is instant and deals 10 percent more, and it summons an Ancestor for 8 seconds (Mac CYwhgHdBAAM 04:56, Realm Gaming 14:09).
- Ascendance: 15 seconds, a 3 minute base cooldown cut to 2 minutes (Mac CYwhgHdBAAM 07:22, Money 06:04, Realm Gaming 20:07, Maxroll's "3 minute cooldown" is the base). Overload damage up 30 percent and every mastery-affected spell overloads once more (Realm Gaming 08:57).
- Call of the Ancestors: Stormkeeper and Ancestral Swiftness each summon an Ancestor, and Lightning Bolt, Lava Burst, Flame Shock, Voltaic Blaze and Chain Lightning each have an 8 percent chance to summon one (BOND 18:48). An Ancestor casts Lava Burst after a single-target spell and Chain Lightning (5 targets) after an AoE spell (Mac gUDeNbgw34I 02:37, O2lXhuipom0 01:09), and casts Elemental Blast when it departs, which gives you the Elemental Blast stat buffs (Final Calling, BOND 15:45, Petko 12:07). When you cast Voltaic Blaze, Ancestors fire one Lava Burst per Flame Shocked mob (BOND 20:34).
- Elemental Blast: raises crit or haste by 7 percent or mastery by 14 percent for 10 seconds, one at random (Realm Gaming 06:30). Inside Ascendance the extra overloads can grant all three from one cast (Mac juty9i35yfY 03:36).
- Tier set (Maxroll's text, BOND's reading): 2-piece "Earth Shock, Elemental Blast and Earthquake damage increased by 25%." 4-piece: when Stormkeeper or Ascendance fades, your next 2 Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning or Lava Burst casts deal 25 percent more damage (Flowing Elements, up to 4 stacks), and each one makes your next Earthquake, Earth Shock or Elemental Blast cost no Maelstrom (Overcharge, 1 stack).
- Defensives: Astral Shift 40 percent for the usual duration on a 1.5 minute cooldown (Mac O2lXhuipom0 15:55). Earth Elemental with Primordial Bond: 15 percent more maximum health while it is out and no taunt (Realm Gaming 12:31). Windveil: 15 percent spell damage reduction for 12 seconds after a successful Wind Shear (Mac O2lXhuipom0 16:48). Earth Shield: a self heal with 9 charges (Javier). Spirit Wolf: a stacking 5 percent damage reduction in Ghost Wolf that costs casts (Mac 16:16). Wind Shear: 12 second cooldown (Realm Gaming 12:51). Spiritwalker's Grace: 15 seconds of casting while moving on a 2 minute cooldown (Realm Gaming 19:03).

## 5. The opener

Single target, Method 12.1.5 (the written standard):
1. Stormkeeper about 3 seconds before the pull.
2. Lava Burst about 1.5 seconds before the pull, timed to land at 0.
3. Ancestral Swiftness.
4. Ascendance with trinket and potion.
5. Lightning Bolt.
6. Lava Burst.
7. The normal rotation.

Single target, Mac 12.1.5 (28 Oct, from Fonnie's and BOND's live logs): Stormkeeper at about 1.5 to 2 seconds before the pull, then the two Stormkeeper Lightning Bolts, then Ascendance with "all of our buttons", then Lava Burst into Elemental Blast three times, then the priority (01:17, 04:11). No pre-pull Lava Burst: "we have an ancestor out that's not doing anything really for us" (02:34). The core rule (02:26): "spending your Storm Keeper before you hit Ascendance." Elemental Blast is the first spender in Ascendance so its three buffs can all land from the first cast (03:20 to 04:00).

Single target, BOND (launch): two openers, "It does not matter which you choose" (08:01). A: Stormkeeper 3 seconds before the pull, Lava Burst 1.5 seconds before, then cooldowns. B, his: Stormkeeper hard cast at about 1 second before the pull, then at the pull Bloodlust, Ancestral Swiftness, Ascendance (macro with potion and trinket), Lava Burst, Stormkeeper Lightning Bolt, Lava Burst, Lightning Bolt, Lava Burst, Lightning Bolt, then the priority (08:20 to 08:58). In his September tier guide the default is Stormkeeper cast with Ascendance so both fade together (03:50), except on single-target vulnerability phases, where Flame Shock goes out before the pull, both Stormkeeper charges go out before Ascendance, then Ascendance, Ancestral Swiftness Lava Burst, Elemental Blast, Lava Burst, Elemental Blast (11:28 to 12:06).

The grading decision for single target: Stormkeeper is cast before the pull or before the pack, and both charges are spent before Ascendance on bosses and on single-target damage phases (Mac 12.1.5, BOND's vulnerability opener, Mac zVjen8YoWJQ 03:30 "Stormkeeper should always be used and consumed before Ascendance"). The Method and BOND launch openers that spend the charges inside Ascendance are not faulted, because Method's 12.1.5 page still prints one and BOND calls the gain "very small" (14:12). A pre-pull Lava Burst is reported, not faulted (14.5).

AoE, Method 12.1.5: Stormkeeper 1.5 seconds before the pull, Ancestral Swiftness, Voltaic Blaze, Ascendance with trinket and potion, Lava Burst, the normal rotation.

AoE, Mythic+ pulls, BOND (the only source with pull-size variants, 11:01, 13:29, 16:17 to 17:26):
- 2 targets: Voltaic Blaze, Stormkeeper, Ancestral Swiftness, Ascendance (trinket and potion), Ancestral Swiftness Lava Burst to consume Purging Flames, Chain Lightning filler.
- 3 targets: Voltaic Blaze, Stormkeeper, Ancestral Swiftness, Ascendance, Ancestral Swiftness Lava Burst, Chain Lightning, Chain Lightning, Earthquake.
- 4+ targets: 1 or 2 Chain Lightnings during the gather, Voltaic Blaze, Stormkeeper, Ancestral Swiftness, Ascendance (potion and trinket), Bloodlust, Ancestral Swiftness Elemental Blast for its buffs ("you only have to do this one time at the beginning of your pulls", 17:10), then Chain Lightning and Earthquake. The reason for the early Maelstrom: "if you start from 0 and go straight into your CDs, sometimes you'll be at 80 Maelstrom and that is not enough to Elemental Blast" (16:52).
- BOND's tier guide adds: Voltaic Blaze goes before the cooldowns "because you want this AoE lava burst available after Ascendance during your Ascendance window" (06:36), and in AoE Stormkeeper and Ascendance are cast together, "I don't recommend" Stormkeeper's charges before Ascendance in AoE (10:58).

Ascendance entry, Mac (gUDeNbgw34I 06:34): "somewhere around the ballpark of 50 Maelstrom before you press it if possible. This is so your first cast inside that window can be an Elemental Blast with Ancestral Swiftness on top of it". "This works in Mythic Plus easily as well as mid-fight in a raid encounter, but doesn't work so much on a raid actual pull opener because you just don't have the Maelstrom yet" (07:17).

Potion and trinkets go with Ascendance in every source that names them (Method, BOND 07:21, Mac O2lXhuipom0 05:36, Realm Gaming 18:33, Money, Saltii). Realm Gaming puts them right after Ancestral Swiftness for its 10 percent. Bloodlust goes with the opening Ascendance (BOND 08:32, 16:35).

## 6. Priority lists

Method 12.1.5, single target, M+ Build (verbatim lines):
1. Stormkeeper. May be held for damage amps or AoE, but ideally not more than 10 seconds.
2. Ancestral Swiftness.
3. Ascendance, with trinket, potion, and Bloodlust.
4. Refresh Voltaic Blaze if Fire Elemental has under 3 seconds left.
5. Refresh Voltaic Blaze with under 6 seconds left.
6. Elemental Blast when within 15 Maelstrom of cap.
7. Lava Burst when there is no Master of the Elements buff.
8. Elemental Blast with Master of the Elements.
9. Lightning Bolt.
(The Raid Build inserts "Lightning Bolt buffed by Stormkeeper, with Master of the Elements" between 7 and 8 and refreshes Flame Shock in place of Voltaic Blaze.)

Method 12.1.5, AoE, M+ Build:
- 2 targets: the single-target list, but Voltaic Blaze on cooldown, and Chain Lightning instead of Lightning Bolt.
- 3 to 5 targets: 1 Stormkeeper (same hold guidance). 2 Ancestral Swiftness. 3 Voltaic Blaze on cooldown. 4 Ascendance, with trinket, potion, and Bloodlust. 5 Lava Burst with Purging Flames, ideally on a Lava Surge proc. 6 Earthquake, when Ancestors are up. 7 Elemental Blast, when no Ancestors are up. 8 Chain Lightning.
- 5+ targets: the 3 to 5 list, but always Earthquake, regardless of Ancestors.

Maxroll 12.1.5, Farseer single target: 1 Stormkeeper on cooldown. 2 Ascendance on cooldown. 3 Ancestral Swiftness on cooldown. 4 Earth Shock or Elemental Blast with Master of the Elements active, or close to overcapping Maelstrom. 5 Lava Burst. 6 Lightning Bolt. 7 Frost Shock on the move.

## 7. Cooldown rules

Order. Stormkeeper, then Ancestral Swiftness, then Ascendance when all three go together (Mac CYwhgHdBAAM 04:27, 09:09). Stormkeeper first because Ancestral Swiftness's Ancestor takes 3 seconds off a Stormkeeper that is on cooldown and nothing off one that is ready (02:39), and because it is "a setup button, so you don't want to be spending an Ascendance global on it" (04:34). Mac gUDeNbgw34I (05:43): "you always cast Stormkeeper before anything." "After Stormkeeper though, the other order of your cooldowns doesn't really matter" (06:22). Every video that names the pair puts Ascendance after Stormkeeper (Realm Gaming 19:46, Kephas 02:04, Saltii 01:12, RampageWoW 0:38, Javier 02:25).

In single target consume both charges before Ascendance (section 5). In AoE cast it with Ascendance (BOND iI2O02l_tE4 14:51, contested, 14.1). When it comes back with Ascendance down, cast it alone and spend both charges (BOND 09:27).

Ancestral Swiftness. On cooldown, "a massive damage loss" to sit on (Mac CYwhgHdBAAM 05:04, Method, Maxroll, BOND 07:06, Petko 17:19, Money 05:58, Javier, Kephas). What the instant goes on: Method says a spell with a cast time and never Earth Shock, BOND says Lava Burst at 2 and 3 targets (to consume Purging Flames) and Elemental Blast at 4+ (for its buffs), Mac says the Elemental Blast at Ascendance entry, Petko says it does not matter (14.10). Ascendance. On cooldown, "as many of those into a dungeon or a fight as possible. That is still the baseline" (Mac CYwhgHdBAAM 01:00). The one override is whether the target survives it (01:16). Mythic+: send it on "a pack of three", "a pack at 50%", "a single target mini boss" (02:04), never into "a pack that is already melting" (01:37). Money (06:23): hold Ascendance up to about 15 seconds to meet Stormkeeper. Enter at about 50 Maelstrom with Elemental Blast first where the pull allows (Mac gUDeNbgw34I 06:34).
