# Elemental Shaman, Midnight Season 2 (12.1 / 12.1.5): the rules as the guides state them

Compiled 2026-10-30 for grading a Farseer Elemental Shaman Mythic+ log in wcl-analyzer. Sources: three written guides (Method, Maxroll, Murlok's top 50) and eighteen YouTube videos found by eight searches over everything uploaded since 2026-06-01 (51 unique hits, selection.md in the session scratchpad records every one and why it is in or out). Eleven videos were read from YouTube's captions, seven were transcribed with Whisper after the caption endpoint rate-limited (their captions arrived on a later retry and were not used), and two were run through Whisper because YouTube had no captions and turned out to hold no speech. Two more were transcribed and then thrown out because they are about "World of Warcraft Forever", a vanilla-style game, not Midnight.

Names are normalized from the auto-captions and from Whisper: "Volcanic Blaze", "Voltic Blast", "Voltaire Blaze" and "VB" are Voltaic Blaze. "Ellie Blast", "Ele Blast" and "EB" are Elemental Blast. "Ascendant", "ascendants" and "a sentence" are Ascendance. "Storm Keeper" and "SK" are Stormkeeper. "mail storm" is Maelstrom. "Ancestral Guidance" in Mac's August guide is Ancestral Swiftness (the slot and the same author's later videos settle it). "grandparents", "the guys" and "ancestors" are the Call of the Ancestors summons. "Kick DR" and "Windvil" are Windveil, the class talent that gives 15 percent spell damage reduction for 12 seconds after a Wind Shear interrupt (Murlok: 47 of 50 take it). "the meatball barrage" is Mac's name for the Lava Burst cast under Purging Flames. "Flowing Elements" and "Overcharge" are the two buffs of the tier 4-piece. Flowing Elements is named by BOND, Petko and Money, Overcharge by BOND and Money.

A figure marked [log] is to be measured from the logs before it is graded, because no source names it. A cut-off marked (grading tolerance) is this rulebook's choice where the sources give only words. A rule marked "count" or "report" goes into the report and is not a per-press fault.

## 2. What changed for Season 2 / 12.1 / 12.1.5

- Farseer replaced Stormbringer for all content. Mac (O2lXhuipom0, 00:33): "the expected meta for pretty much everything is Farseer over Stormbringer, which is basically the exact opposite of where we started Midnight." Why (00:42): "Both Tempest and our mastery overloads took a big nerf moving into 12.1 and that naturally hurt Stormbringer a lot". (01:09): "Ancestors Chain Lightning now hits five targets instead of three". BOND (01:18): "forget about Stormbringer. We are Farseer for all content." Petko (00:50): "Do not play Stormbringer." Murlok: 50 of 50.
- Power moved out of Ascendance into the baseline. BOND (00:48): "they removed power from our Ascendance, and they gave it to us as damage outside of Ascendance." Mac (O2lXhuipom0, 02:02): "The 12.1 tuning did push more of our damage into our baseline kit." Maxroll's changelog: Ascendance's overload bonus "30% (was 75%)" (its summary still says 150 percent, a contradiction on the same page).
- Aftershock is out, Tectonic Collapse is in, because the tier set gives free spenders. Mac (O2lXhuipom0, 03:39). BOND (01:23): "Aftershock is out. No more aftershock. We're running Tectonic Collapse."
- Power of the Maelstrom was reworked: Lightning Bolt or Chain Lightning has "a 15% chance to empower your next lava burst by 20%" (Mac, O2lXhuipom0, 04:03). Nothing to manage.
- Primordial Wave is removed (Maxroll). Icefury is gone (Money, A6w0oYK6GnI, 01:10). Purging Flames brings back the Lava Burst that hits every Flame Shocked target (Petko, 09:40).
- Earthquake can be cast on the target or at a location, and both deal the same damage (Petko 16:11 to 16:21, who plays location). Method prefers targeted ("Generally speaking, I prefer targeted Earthquake to cursor"), Realm Gaming describes his targeted setup (07:44). A choice, not a patch change. [log] Read the placement mode from the cast events before grading hits.
- Ancestral Swiftness replaced Nature's Swiftness for Farseer: the next cast is instant, deals 10 percent more and summons an Ancestor for 8 seconds (Realm Gaming 14:09, Mac CYwhgHdBAAM 04:56).
- Lava Burst holds 3 charges with Farseer (Mac zVjen8YoWJQ 00:44, Realm Gaming 20:46).
- The Season 2 tier set (section 11) is "probably the biggest thing in 12.1 that actually changes how the spec feels to play" (Mac, O2lXhuipom0, 09:41) and "the only min-max part" (Petko, 07:50).
- 12.1.5 (released before 14 Oct 2026): Method and Maxroll are updated to it. The only video that names it is Mac's 28 Oct opener, which changes the single-target opener from last season's (section 5). No source lists 12.1.5 tuning for Elemental.

## 4. The mechanics

- Maelstrom: Elemental Blast costs 90 (Realm Gaming 06:26 "I believe", BOND iI2O02l_tE4 12:54 firm), Earthquake 60 (Javier 04:16, the only source). The cap is not named by any source. BOND treats 123 as overcapping and casts a spender at 117 "trying not to overcap" (iI2O02l_tE4 05:47, 06:03). Swelling Maelstrom raises the cap and 47 of 50 take it. [log] Read the cap from the resource events (the max field) and both costs from the first Elemental Blast and Earthquake resource events before grading overcap or "a spender was castable". This is the one place the cap carries the tag, rules 9 and 10 point here.
- Maelstrom is generated by Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning (per target hit), Lava Burst, Flame Shock ticks and overloads (Realm Gaming 03:28, 16:58, 17:12). Ancestors generate none (15:29).
- Master of the Elements: a Lava Burst cast makes the next spell deal 15 percent more (Mac zVjen8YoWJQ 00:06, gUDeNbgw34I 08:43). Almost any spell consumes it, Flame Shock and Voltaic Blaze included (gUDeNbgw34I 01:51).
- Lava Burst: 3 charges with Farseer, about 8 seconds a charge (zVjen8YoWJQ 00:44). Lava Surge: Flame Shock ticks reset a charge and make the next Lava Burst instant (Realm Gaming 02:26). Lava Burst always crits on a Flame Shocked target (Realm Gaming 04:31). [log] Warcraft Logs records no charge count. Read the recharge from the shortest spacing of three consecutive hard casts, rebuild the charges from cast events and Lava Surge applybuff events, and treat rules 23, 24 and 57 as approximate. The Ascendance press itself fires a Lava Burst at up to 6 enemies and the Ancestors cast Lava Burst too, so every Lava Burst rule reads player-sourced casts only and skips the one on the Ascendance applybuff timestamp.
- Flame Shock: 18 seconds, pandemic window 6 seconds or less, a refresh at 7 seconds resets to 18 and loses the remainder (Realm Gaming 21:54). Ascendance applies Flame Shock at 50 percent to up to 6 targets and makes a Flame Shock applied inside it last 36 seconds (Realm Gaming 08:14, 10:38, Javier 08:29 "newly applied ones last double"), so the pandemic window on that debuff is about 10.8 seconds. Any Flame Shocked target takes 10 percent more damage from you (BOND iI2O02l_tE4 11:32).
- Voltaic Blaze replaces Flame Shock on the bars and applies it to the targets it hits (Realm Gaming 05:40). Its cooldown is about 8 seconds (Mac zVjen8YoWJQ 04:46) after Crackling Fury and Flames of the Cauldron take "a total of 7 and 1/2 seconds off" (gUDeNbgw34I 04:13). It grants Purging Flames.
- Purging Flames: the next Lava Burst also fires at 5 additional Flame Shocked targets at 80 percent effectiveness with reduced Maelstrom generation, and Lava Burst deals 10 percent more (Realm Gaming's tooltip read, 23:09). Casting Voltaic Blaze again with Purging Flames unspent overwrites it (Mac zVjen8YoWJQ 06:08). [log] No source names the duration of Purging Flames, Lava Surge, Flowing Elements or Overcharge. Read each from applybuff to removebuff with no consuming cast, since each decides a fault or a pass (rules 28, 30, 56, 60).
- Stormkeeper: 60 second cooldown, cast time, 2 charges, the next 2 Lightning Bolts (150 percent more damage) or Chain Lightnings (an overload on every target) are instant (Realm Gaming 15:52). Every Ancestor summoned takes 3 seconds off its cooldown (Mac gUDeNbgw34I 05:56, BOND 18:43), so its effective cooldown is "40 to 45 seconds" (Mac CYwhgHdBAAM 03:08, Money 7HOSKorzVpw 06:09 "about 45 seconds"). [log] A 5-second range is 47 against 52 possible casts over a 35-minute key, so read the effective cooldown from the Ancestor summon events (3 seconds each) or from the shortest observed gaps before counting.
- Ancestral Swiftness: 30 second cooldown, off the global cooldown, the next cast is instant and deals 10 percent more, and it summons an Ancestor for 8 seconds (Mac CYwhgHdBAAM 04:56, Realm Gaming 14:09).
- Ascendance: 15 seconds. The cooldown depends on the choice node: 2 minutes with First Ascendant (Murlok 38 of 50, Mac CYwhgHdBAAM 07:22 "a two-minute cooldown, which is a three-minute base", Money 7HOSKorzVpw 06:04, Realm Gaming 20:07), 3 minutes with Preeminence (Method names the pair, Money A6w0oYK6GnI 02:27 "three minute Ascendance is good as well and it will see play if fight timers are favorable", Javier 01:05 and 02:25, Maxroll's "3 minute cooldown" is the base). Overload damage up 30 percent and every mastery-affected spell overloads once more (Realm Gaming 08:57). [log] Read the node from the combatant info, or the shortest gap between Ascendance casts, before counting possible casts or grading the window length.
- Call of the Ancestors: Stormkeeper and Ancestral Swiftness each summon an Ancestor, and Lightning Bolt, Lava Burst, Flame Shock, Voltaic Blaze and Chain Lightning each have an 8 percent chance to summon one (BOND 18:48). An Ancestor casts Lava Burst after a single-target spell and Chain Lightning (5 targets) after an AoE spell (Mac gUDeNbgw34I 02:37, O2lXhuipom0 01:09), and casts Elemental Blast when it departs, which gives you the Elemental Blast stat buffs (Final Calling, BOND 15:45, Petko 12:07). When you cast Voltaic Blaze, Ancestors fire one Lava Burst per Flame Shocked mob (BOND 20:34). The 8-second lifetime comes from Realm Gaming (low) and Mac. [log] Confirm it from summon to despawn events before rebuilding "Ancestors up at the press" for rules 15 and 16.
- Elemental Blast: raises crit or haste by 7 percent or mastery by 14 percent for 10 seconds, one at random (Realm Gaming 06:30). Inside Ascendance the extra overloads can grant all three from one cast (Mac juty9i35yfY 03:36).
- Tier set (Maxroll's text, BOND's reading): 2-piece "Earth Shock, Elemental Blast and Earthquake damage increased by 25%." 4-piece: when Stormkeeper or Ascendance fades, your next 2 Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning or Lava Burst casts deal 25 percent more damage (Flowing Elements, up to 4 stacks), and each one makes your next Earthquake, Earth Shock or Elemental Blast cost no Maelstrom (Overcharge, 1 stack). ## 5. The opener

Single target, Method 12.1.5 (the written standard):
1. Stormkeeper about 3 seconds before the pull.
2. Lava Burst about 1.5 seconds before the pull, timed to land at 0.
3. Ancestral Swiftness.
4. Ascendance with trinket and potion.
5. Lightning Bolt.
6. Lava Burst.
7. The normal rotation.

Single target, Mac 12.1.5 (28 Oct, from Fonnie's log, a GG log and BOND's add phase): Stormkeeper at about 1.5 to 2 seconds before the pull, then the two Stormkeeper Lightning Bolts, then Ascendance with "all of our buttons", then Lava Burst into Elemental Blast three times, then the priority (01:17, 04:11). No pre-pull Lava Burst: "we have an ancestor out that's not doing anything really for us" (02:34). The core rule (02:26): "spending your Storm Keeper before you hit Ascendance." ## 7. Cooldown rules

Order. Stormkeeper, then Ancestral Swiftness, then Ascendance when all three go together (Mac CYwhgHdBAAM 04:27, 09:09). Stormkeeper first because Ancestral Swiftness's Ancestor takes 3 seconds off a Stormkeeper that is on cooldown and nothing off one that is ready (02:39), and because it is "a setup button, so you don't want to be spending an Ascendance global on it" (04:34). Mac gUDeNbgw34I (05:43): "you always cast Stormkeeper before anything." "After Stormkeeper though, the other order of your cooldowns doesn't really matter" (06:22). Every video that names the order inside the pair puts Ascendance after Stormkeeper (Realm Gaming 19:46, Kephas 02:04, Saltii 01:12, RampageWoW 0:38). Javier gives the cadence (02:25 "Storm Keeper, 1 minute, 2 minute and on the 3rd minute pair them together") and no order inside the pair.

Stormkeeper. On cooldown. Hold it for Ascendance up to about 10 seconds (Method "ideally not more than 10 seconds", BOND 06:58 and 19:21 "Generally, you don't want to hold any longer than 10 seconds, or you're going to risk losing cooldowns", Mac CYwhgHdBAAM 02:30, Mac O2lXhuipom0 17:25, Money 7HOSKorzVpw 06:23). Past 10 seconds the hold is the loss the sources name, and the lost cast is what a log shows. In keys Mac is lenient: "10 seconds late on a Storm Keeper cast. That's not necessarily a mistake anymore" (CYwhgHdBAAM 03:41), "in Keys, let it drift a bit" (04:08). Never cast it inside Ascendance (Mac CYwhgHdBAAM 04:34, gUDeNbgw34I 05:47). Pre-cast it before the pull (every opener). On an add fight, pre-cast it before the adds (Mac juty9i35yfY 02:14). In single target consume both charges before Ascendance (section 5). In AoE either order passes, with Ascendance or before it, report which (BOND iI2O02l_tE4 14:51 against Mac, contested, 14.1). When it comes back with Ascendance down, cast it alone and spend both charges (BOND 09:27).

Ancestral Swiftness. On cooldown, "a massive damage loss" to sit on (Mac CYwhgHdBAAM 05:04, Method, Maxroll, BOND 07:06, Petko 17:19, Money 05:58, Javier, Kephas). The one hold: when Stormkeeper is 10 seconds or less from ready, or Ascendance is about to come up, hold it and pair them (Mac 04:12, BOND 11:58 and 18:33). Mac gives no figure for the Ascendance side. What the instant goes on: Method says a spell with a cast time and never Earth Shock, BOND says Lava Burst at 2 and 3 targets (to consume Purging Flames) and Elemental Blast at 4+ (for its buffs), Mac says the Elemental Blast at Ascendance entry, Petko says it does not matter and spends it on Chain Lightning on purpose (14.10). It is off the global cooldown and costs no cast (Mac 04:39, Kephas).

Ascendance. On cooldown, "as many of those into a dungeon or a fight as possible. That is still the baseline" (Mac CYwhgHdBAAM 01:00). The one override is whether the target survives it (01:16). Mythic+: send it on "a pack of three", "a pack at 50%", "a single target mini boss" (02:04), never into "a pack that is already melting" (01:37). Waiting for the perfect pull ends a dungeon with two fewer Ascendance casts than it should have had (02:13). Planned high-key routes are the exception (02:19). Mac O2lXhuipom0 (14:11): "The biggest mistake I see people tend to make is holding Ascendance for so long you lose an entire use or two of it over the course of a dungeon." Money (06:23): hold Ascendance up to about 15 seconds to meet Stormkeeper, the only Mythic+ hold figure, from a source weighted low for rotation. Enter at about 50 Maelstrom with Elemental Blast first where the pull allows (Mac gUDeNbgw34I 06:34). Potion, on-use trinkets and Bloodlust go with it. Its cooldown is 2 minutes with First Ascendant and 3 with Preeminence (section 4).

Voltaic Blaze inside Ascendance: BOND yes (iI2O02l_tE4 07:38, uCyFmdCIIvA 17:37, 20:29 "Purging Flames is a lot of damage, and casting Voltaic Blaze, Ancestors will also fire 1 Lava Burst per Flame Shocked Mob"), Mac's August guide no (O2lXhuipom0 19:23, the Ancestors would cast Lava Burst instead of Chain Lightning, "the priority is really just fit as many chain lightnings into that window as possible"). Contested, 14.7, not graded.

Fire Elemental, Storm Elemental, Liquid Magma Totem, Totemic Recall: no source gives a rule. BOND times a Flame Shock reapplication around Fire Elemental (07:28 "after Ascendance ends, but before Fire Elemental spawns", 09:27 "right before your Fire Elemental should despawn") and Method refreshes Voltaic Blaze "if Fire Elemental has under 3 seconds left". Whether Fire Elemental is a cast or an Ascendance effect is settled from the log (section 4). Report Fire Elemental against its cooldown where it has one and the Flame Shock refresh around it, no fault.

- Never overcap. Method: Elemental Blast "when within 15 Maelstrom of cap". ## 11. The tier set

The text is in section 4. How to play it (BOND iI2O02l_tE4, Mac gUDeNbgw34I 07:30 to 09:59, Mac O2lXhuipom0 09:56 to 11:37, Mac CYwhgHdBAAM 06:07 to 06:56, Petko 07:55 to 09:12, Money):
- When Stormkeeper's charges are spent or Ascendance fades, Flowing Elements lands: 2 stacks per fade, 4 when Stormkeeper and Ascendance fade together (BOND 13:56). It stacks to 4, and a fade that lands while 3 or 4 stacks are still held throws stacks away: "never ever overcap on the tier set" (Petko 08:43), "in order to avoid you overcapping on the bonus" (09:00). Each Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning or Lava Burst consumes one stack, deals 25 percent more, and grants Overcharge, which stacks to 1 and makes the next Earthquake, Earth Shock or Elemental Blast free.
- Alternate builder, spender, builder, spender until the stacks are gone. Two builders in a row with Overcharge up throws a free spender away. Mac (gUDeNbgw34I 08:17) calls it the mistake he sees most, "and honestly one I struggled with as well". BOND (02:01): "if you have 2 stacks of Flowing Elements, and you cast 2 Lightning Bolts, or 2 Lava Bursts, or 2 Chain Lightnings in a row, you will waste 1 stack of Overcharge". Petko (09:00): "never cast back-to-back double generator".
- Lava Burst is the builder of choice, because its Master of the Elements lands on the free spender (Mac gUDeNbgw34I 08:34, CYwhgHdBAAM 06:39, BOND 05:14). Do not wait for a charge: "If lava burst isn't up, don't wait and you definitely don't overwrite a tier set buff trying to wait for a charge to come back" (gUDeNbgw34I 08:59). Hold 2 charges for the fade when you can (zVjen8YoWJQ 02:50).
- Dump Maelstrom before the fade (Mac O2lXhuipom0 11:07). Inside the window, when near the cap or waiting on a charge, cast a paid spender between the free ones, back-to-back spenders are fine (gUDeNbgw34I 09:21, CYwhgHdBAAM 06:44, BOND 09:06). Overcap beats a Lava Burst-buffed spender (BOND 05:58).
- The munch at the fade: a hard-cast Lightning Bolt started with "roughly less than a second" of Ascendance left lands after the fade, consumes a stack at once, and a Lava Burst straight after it wastes an Overcharge (BOND 04:18). Plan 2 to 3 seconds before the fade which cast lands inside (04:06).
- The last Overcharge can be held, and buffed by a Lava Burst later (BOND 10:13, 10:27).
- After a hard-cast Lava Burst on the last stack, Overcharge lands about 0.2 to 0.3 seconds later, so an Elemental Blast queued at once under 90 Maelstrom is refused (BOND 12:42). Advanced, no grading.
- Money's pre-launch read (A6w0oYK6GnI 06:58): the first Overcharge often survived the first spender, "two free elemental blasts". Settled from the log before the waste rules are graded (section 4).
- In AoE the free spenders are Earthquakes, with Chain Lightning as the builder, and a Purging Flames Lava Burst taken when it lines up (BOND 08:32 to 09:18, Mac zVjen8YoWJQ 07:13).

## 15. AUDIT RULES

How to read these rules: one sentence each, tags name the sources, "count" or "report" means it goes into the report and is not a per-press fault, "(grading tolerance)" marks a cut-off this rulebook chose where the sources give only words, "contested" points to section 14, [log] means measure it before grading, "low weight" marks a fault carried by one source or by sources section 1 weights low.

Targets. Two counts are used and each rule says which. The hit count is the distinct enemies a cast damaged, read from its damage events, and the hit-based checks use it: rule 17's Chain Lightning that damaged one enemy, rule 18's Earthquake hits per tick, rule 76's Flame Shock coverage. The live count is the enemies in combat within range at the press, which in the log is the distinct hostile units the party damaged in the 3 seconds before the press that were alive at it (grading tolerance on the 3 seconds), checked against the shaman's nearest Chain Lightning or Earthquake hit count. The live count decides every choice check: rules 14, 15, 16, 17 (which filler), 25, 26, 29, 30, 42, 43, 45, 53 and 75. "Spread or moving" in rule 16 is read as the player's Chain Lightning or Earthquake within 5 seconds either side hitting fewer than the live count (grading tolerance), otherwise the Elemental Blast is not exempt. A pull starts at the first party damage event on the pack.
