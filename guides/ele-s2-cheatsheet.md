# Elemental Shaman (Farseer) Mythic+, Season 2: the one page

The keyboard version of guides/ele-s2-rotation.md. The rules behind every line are in guides/ele-s2-rules.md.

## Cooldowns
- Order: Stormkeeper, Ancestral Swiftness, Ascendance.
- Stormkeeper on cooldown. Hold it for Ascendance at most 10 s. Never cast it inside Ascendance.
- Ancestral Swiftness every 30 s. Only hold it when Stormkeeper is under 10 s away.
- Ascendance on cooldown, on anything that will live 15 s. Potion, trinket and lust go on the same press.

## Boss opener
```
1. Stormkeeper at 2 s on the pull timer
2. Lightning Bolt
3. Lightning Bolt
4. Ascendance (macro: trinkets, potion, Ancestral Swiftness)
5. Lava Burst, Elemental Blast
6. Lava Burst, Elemental Blast
7. Lava Burst, Elemental Blast
8. Single target loop
```

## Trash pull opener
```
1. Target the priority mob, 1 or 2 Chain Lightnings while the tank gathers
2. Voltaic Blaze
3. Stormkeeper
4. Ascendance (macro)
5. 2 to 3 mobs: Lava Burst (instant). 4 or more: one Elemental Blast
6. Chain Lightning, Chain Lightning
7. AoE loop
```

## Single target loop
```
1. Near Maelstrom cap: Elemental Blast
2. Master of the Elements up: Elemental Blast
3. No buff: Lava Burst
4. Lightning Bolt
```
Flame Shock (Voltaic Blaze) at 6 s or less, never onto the buff. Never two Lava Bursts in a row. Keep one Lava Burst charge in hand.

## AoE loop, 3 or more mobs
```
1. Voltaic Blaze on cooldown
2. Near cap: Earthquake
3. Lava Burst only when instant and only with Purging Flames up, Earthquake right after it
4. Earthquake (Elemental Blast at 3 or 4 if no Ancestor is out)
5. Chain Lightning
```
2 mobs: the single target loop with Chain Lightning as the filler, hard-cast Lava Burst freely.

## Tier window (Stormkeeper spent, or Ascendance fades)
```
Dump Maelstrom before the fade, then:
builder (Lava Burst if a charge is up), free spender, builder, free spender
```
Never two builders in a row. Near the cap, a paid spender in between is fine.

## Never
- Overcap Maelstrom.
- Hard-cast Lava Burst at 4 or more mobs, except to clear Purging Flames before Voltaic Blaze returns.
- Press Voltaic Blaze with Purging Flames unspent.
- Elemental Blast at 5 or more mobs. Earthquake at 2.
- Hold Ascendance for a better pull.
- Die with Astral Shift up.

## The macro
```
#showtooltip Ascendance
/use 13
/use 14
/use Light's Potential
/cast Ancestral Swiftness
/cast Ascendance
```
Slot 13 is the top trinket, 14 the bottom. Keep a separate key for Ancestral Swiftness between Ascendances.
